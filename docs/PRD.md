# PRD

## Product Summary
VehiMeet is a role-based vehicle booking platform with customer, partner, and admin workflows. The platform covers booking creation, KYC review, partner assignment, live tracking, and operational oversight.

## MVP Scope
- Authentication for all three roles
- Vehicle browsing and partner vehicle management
- Booking creation, detail view, and cancellation
- Partner accept, reject, start, and complete actions
- Live tracking updates
- Admin approval and assignment workflows
- Basic KYC session lifecycle

## Future Scope
- Payments
- Ratings
- Coupons
- Invoices
- Advanced analytics
